What I fly

Six services. One flight crew.

Every job gets a certified pilot, a flight plan filed before takeoff, and footage or data delivered within a set timeframe — not "when it's ready."

SOLCELLER

Solar panel fault detection

Drone thermography is the most effective method for quickly scanning large panel arrays and locating hidden defects without physical contact. When a cell or string is faulty, electrical resistance rises — generating heat that a high-resolution thermal camera instantly captures as glowing hotspots.

EJENDOMME

Drone inspection of buildings

Drone inspection of buildings is a fast, safe and cost-effective way to assess the condition of roofs, facades and other hard-to-reach structures.

BYGGERI

Construction progress monitoring

Scheduled flights that follow a construction site from groundbreaking to handover in one continuous visual record.

KORTLÆGNING

Surveying & Orthophotos

Georeferenced maps, volume calculations and elevation models built from precise flight data.

INDSATS

Fire fighting, rescue and search

Aerial overview of fire fronts, missing persons and rescue operations — giving emergency crews real-time situational awareness.

LÆKAGESPORING

District heating leak detection

Efficient leak detection with drone thermography minimises heat loss, reduces CO₂ emissions and prevents costly secondary damage. Drones equipped with high-resolution thermal cameras can locate leaks in buried district heating pipes quickly and precisely without interrupting the heat supply.
